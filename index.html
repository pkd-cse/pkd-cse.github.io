Biography

Welcome to my academic portal. I am a faculty member in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Sylhet Agricultural University, Sylhet, Bangladesh. My work focuses on teaching undergraduate courses, mentoring student projects, and conducting research in computing and agricultural applications.

Research Interests

  • Bioinformatics & Agricultural Informatics: Smart farming, crop disease detection using computer vision.
  • Machine Learning & Data Science: Predictive modeling, neural networks.
  • Internet of Things (IoT): Automated systems for precision agriculture.
